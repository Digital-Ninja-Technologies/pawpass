Payments and escrow

How escrow protects you

2 minute read · updated 6 Nov

When you pay for a visit or a puppy, PawPass holds the money. The partner is paid only after you say it went well.

  1. 1You payCard, bank transfer or USSD. The money goes to PawPass, not the partner.
  2. 2The visit happensGo to your booking or collect your puppy as agreed.
  3. 3You confirmTap Confirm visit. If you do not, we ask you after 24 hours.
  4. 4The partner is paidPayouts run every Monday.
Something wrong? Open a problem before you confirm. The payout waits until our team decides.
