Layout, spacing & radius

  • Screen side padding 20 (onboarding 24); content starts 52 from top, below the OS status bar
  • Spacing scale 4 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 14 · 16 · 18 · 22; stack gap 14–18 between sections
  • Radius: pill buttons and chips = height ÷ 2; inputs 16; cards 18–24; photos 14–22
  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44; primary buttons 52–54 tall
  • Bottom tab bar 80 tall (white, 1px top border); sticky action footers pad 26 for the home indicator
  • Icons: 24px line icons, 1.8 stroke, round caps (a Lucide-style set fits)
  • Text contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every surface shown

Shared componentsBottom tab bar is one component (TabBar) with an active prop. Back buttons, step progress, sticky action footers, list rows and badges repeat on every screen: build each once.
Content rulesNames, prices and ratings shown are sample data. Photos are free Unsplash stock for the mockup and get replaced by user uploads in the app. Use the pet tints as loading and fallback colours.
Privacy in the UIPhone numbers stay masked in chat and lost & found. Health records are private by default and shared only through time-limited links.
