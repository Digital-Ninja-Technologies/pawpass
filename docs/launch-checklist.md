## 2. What the app ships with

- **Offline:** passport, schedule, reminders and notifications show the last saved copy with a "saved at" banner when there is no connection. Nothing is queued or written offline.
- **Honest states:** every list has loading, empty and error states. A failed load is never shown as "nothing here".
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA):** 44pt tap targets, labelled controls, text contrast, text scale to 1.3, tested at 320×568. Guideline tests live in `test/accessibility_test.dart`.
- **Analytics:** six events only (`app_open`, `booking_started`, `checkout_started`, `share_started`, `partner_started`, `help_opened`). No personal data, no ad identifiers, no third party. Everything else in the success metrics is computed from the database (`admin_launch_metrics()`), shown on the admin dashboard.
- **Icons:** generated by `tools/make_store_icons.py` into the Android and iOS projects and `store/`.

## 3. Store listing copy

**Name:** PawPass
**Subtitle / short description (80):** Your pet's health passport, vets, and trusted care in Nigeria.

**Full description:**

PawPass keeps everything about your pet in one place.

- Health passport: vaccines, treatments and vet records, ready to share with a vet, a groomer or a new owner.
- Reminders before a vaccine or treatment is due.
- Book vets, groomers, trainers and boarding from verified partners, with payment held safely until you confirm the visit.
- Find an emergency vet near you.
- Adopt or buy from verified shelters and breeders. Payment for a purchase stays in escrow until handover.
- Lost-pet alerts and QR tags.

Partners (vets, service providers, breeders, shelters) can apply inside the app, get verified, and are paid out weekly.

**Category:** Lifestyle (iOS), Lifestyle or Pets (Google Play)
**Age rating:** decide with care. People can now post, comment publicly and message each other (after the pet's owner accepts a request), as well as chat about lost pets, bookings and listings. Stores treat user generated content as at least 12+ (iOS) or Teen (Google Play), and expect reporting, blocking and a way to remove content. Those exist: report a post, comment or person, block, and admin moderation. Do not submit as 4+.

## 4. Privacy and data safety answers

| Data | Collected | Why | Shared |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, phone | Yes | Account, bookings, receipts | Only with a partner you book, name only |
| Approximate location | Only while the app is open and only if you allow it | Sort emergency vets by distance | No, not stored |
| Photos | Only photos you pick or take | Pet photos, KYC documents for partners | Pet photos with a listing you create; KYC documents only with PawPass staff |
| Pet health records | Yes | The passport | Only through a share link you create |
| Payment | Handled by Paystack; the app never sees card numbers | Payments, payouts | Paystack |
| Analytics events | Six anonymous event names | Measure monthly active users | No |

Data is encrypted in transit. Users can ask for their account to be deleted from Privacy and sharing; the request goes to support. Apple (and Google Play) expect deletion inside the app itself, so build a real delete before submitting. Privacy policy and terms URLs must be live before submission (publish them at `apps/web`).

## 5. Permissions

- **Camera / photo library:** pet photos, partner documents, QR scanning.
- **Location (when in use, approximate on Android):** nearest emergency vet.
- **Internet:** required; declared in the main Android manifest.
- **Notifications:** reminders and booking updates.

The iOS usage strings are in `ios/Runner/Info.plist`.

## 7. Known limits

- Account deletion is a support request, not an in app delete. This is the most likely store review problem.
- The `pt_` payment path is covered by unit tests with fakes, not by a live Paystack run. The community SQL is applied but its behaviour test (`supabase/tests/community_interactions.test.sql`) has not been run.
- A first start with no network and no saved copy cannot tell whether you have finished onboarding.
- Not tested on physical devices in this environment.
- Store assets are the paw mark on the brand colour; replace with final artwork if design supplies it.
